Getting Started with UniLift

UniLift simplifies the complex Ghanaian university course selection process. No more manual calculations or guessing cutoff points.

The 3-Step Process

1

Input Your WASSCE Results

Select your Core and Elective subjects from our dropdown menu. Enter your grades (A1 - F9) exactly as they appear on your result slip.

2

Smart Aggregate Calculation

Our algorithm automatically identifies your Best Six subjects (3 Cores + 3 Electives) to generate your aggregate, ensuring you meet the specific calculation standards of Ghanaian institutions.

3

Payment to unlock results

After submitting your results, you can unlock your personalized course recommendations by making a small payment.

4

Real-time Database Matching

We query our database of updated cutoff points for KNUST, UG, UCC, and more to see which programs align with your performance.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does UniLift work?

UniLift simplifies the university course selection process by automatically calculating your aggregate and matching you with eligible programs based on your WASSCE results.

Which universities are supported?

Currently, we support KNUST, University of Ghana (Legon), UCC, UDS, and several technical universities.

Does an aggregate of 6 guarantee admission?

While UniLift checks eligibility based on cutoff points, final admission depends on the university's competitive intake for that year.
